# Field Survey Storyworks port

`USPFieldSurveyComponent` is a separate native port of `Reference/Original/source/field-story.js`, distinct from the nine-case Long Debt campaign. It reads the 19 source world identities from `Data/Worlds.json` and reproduces the source biome-to-survey descriptions and profiles. A record is created per world and saved through `USPSFieldSurveySaveGame` in the `SpacePatriotFieldSurveys` slot.

Each survey starts with an arrival at the world-specific site. The player must be on foot, outside bridge walk, and within **300 metres** of the site (the original `< .3 km` test). Arrival adds the expedition briefing to that world's journal. A successful ecology scan advances the objective even if it happened before arrival. The scan triggers one Spellworks beacon at the site; glacial worlds use `frost`, all other types use `arcane`. Gas worlds complete at that uplink, while solid worlds require a successful `surface-sampler` collection at the site. Completion archives the source field-report journal line. Replayed scans, arrivals, samples, and resumed saves do not replay the beacon or completion.

The world controller must call `ActivateWorld(worldId, actualSurveySiteLocation)` with a site produced by Worldworks. This component deliberately does not invent a coordinate from the incomplete terrain port. Feed actual player position, walking state, and bridge-walk state to `UpdatePlayerContext` after movement. Call `NotifyWorldScanned` only after the real ecology scan succeeds and `NotifyFieldSample` only after the real sampler succeeds. When loading a legacy ship save, pass its already-scanned world IDs into `NotifyWorldScanned` before the player enters those sites. `OnBeaconPulse` or `TakePendingBeaconPulses` must be connected to Spellworks rendering; the queue retains at most eight pulses, matching the browser source. `GetCurrentStatus` and `GetJournal` supply the field-contract UI. Gameplay events and the survey component must run on the authoritative game actor when multiplayer is implemented.

`SpacePatriot.FieldSurvey.SourceParity` tests all 19 source world records are available, the arrival gates and centimetre/metre conversion, pre-arrival scans, Earth/ice/gas branches, correct beacon presets and eight-pulse queue limit, sampler source and location gates, per-world isolation, journal text, duplicate rejection, and a compact save-memory roundtrip. Passing this test validates the state machine. The playable map still needs actual survey-site actors, ecology scanner and sampler event wiring, a visible Spellworks pulse, field-contract UI, and an integrated save transaction with the rest of the game.
