# Playable Blueprint graph slice

`BP_KestrelFlyable` now owns a `SPVesselSystemsComponent` and has authored Event Graph wiring. `Event Tick` passes the flight pawn's live power, hull, heat, thrust, cruise and shield state into `StepSystems`, then applies engine factor, ship heat, shield level and cruise cancellation back to the flight pawn. Engine damage or disabled power affects flight speed, acceleration and turn response. The graph has four player action events: **F1** selects Travel power allocation, **F2** selects Combat, **F3** cycles the four MFD pages, and **F4** dims the display. A dark-backed, screen-anchored MFD shows live NAV, SYSTEMS, POWER and MISSION data in flight and cockpit views. Its previous/next, Travel, Combat and Dim buttons invoke the same vessel APIs. Press **Tab** to show the pointer for those buttons, then **Tab** to return to mouse flight control. **V** moves the camera and MFD into the cockpit view. The old `CockpitReadout` TextRender remains as a hidden data mirror for Blueprint inspection; it no longer floats in the world.

`BP_WorldRuntime` owns Society, StoryCampaign and FieldSurvey components. Its Event Graph receives player input: **F5** starts the `water` quest in the original Storyworks graph, and **F6** saves society, campaign and field surveys in sequence. Component `BeginPlay` loads existing state and advances society time offline. The mission MFD page reads the live current story node after F5.

`Tools/BuildShipGraph.py` invokes an Editor-only C++ graph builder (`SpacePatriotBlueprintTools`). It refuses to overwrite unknown Tick wiring, rebuilds only its tagged nodes, compiles, saves and verifies the actual K2 call/event links. `Validation/ship-blueprint-graph.json` records the last structural check. `Tools/ValidateBlueprintRuntime.py` spawns the compiled Blueprint in an unsaved editor world and exercises the MFD, vessel preset and campaign link; `Validation/blueprint-runtime.json` records its checks.

The display is a functional screen-anchored UMG panel, not the final cockpit mesh/material screen treatment. These controls make one Kestrel and the first Storyworks quest accessible. They do not complete the full campaign interaction UI, NPC dialogue, combat, world traversal or the full game port.
